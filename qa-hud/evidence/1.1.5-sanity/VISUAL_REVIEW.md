# Rendered GUI sanity review

The inspected images are direct Minecraft render-target captures, with no desktop content. Inspection covered the combined settings root, protected SSO guest page, current Stackables gameplay page, MapStitch client categories, opposite-corner atlas/map HUD placement, same-corner atlas placement, same-bottom-corner ordinary-map placement, a Nether tile at a stale-center regression case, and the empty-atlas message after a filled scene. The automated fixture also checks native widget identities, labels, permissions and geometry on every recorded scene.

The map stays on its configured side independently of Tool Pouch text. On a shared top-left corner, details appear beneath the atlas; on a shared bottom-left corner, details remain above the map without overlap. All 14 original settings pages render, and the root buttons read `Configure...` while their category titles retain the original mod names. The empty-atlas scene clears its previous frame and displays the original map-source guidance.

A first-login offline-chat trust toast and the vanilla movement tutorial are visible in some early screenshots. These are vanilla fixture overlays, not suite overlap failures. Upstream SSO 2.10.0 also warns that its optional XP-bottle/Mending-description rename resource packs have incompatible metadata (`pack_format: 15`). Its gameplay settings and operation tests are separate from these cosmetic packs. The older private official SSO 2.9.14 input also had root pack format 15 but did not include these optional packs. No upstream binary was changed during this QA.
