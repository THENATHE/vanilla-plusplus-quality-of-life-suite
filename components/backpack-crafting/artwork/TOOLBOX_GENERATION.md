# Toolbox sprites

Generated and edited with the built-in imagegen tool. Current originals: `iron_toolbox-vanilla-generated.png` and `netherite_toolbox-vanilla-generated.png`. Earlier hammer/diamond concepts are retained only as art history, outside shipped resources.

Shipped sprites are nearest-neighbor samples at exactly **16×16**, reduced without dithering to **12 colors or fewer**, with alpha restricted to **0 or 255**. Existing `iron_hammer.png` and `diamond_hammer.png` texture names preserve model/item identities; the latter now depicts the netherite toolbox. `iron_toolbox-vanilla-preview.png` and `netherite_toolbox-vanilla-preview.png` enlarge the actual shipped pixels using nearest-neighbor scaling.

## Prompt set

Iron edit: simplify the earlier toolbox into an authentic vanilla Minecraft inventory icon. Preserve the squat rectangular metal chest, small raised handle, dark latch and brown wood inset; use a strict 16×16 logical grid, whole solid-color cells, front-facing silhouette with only a small suggestion of depth, restrained iron/wood palette and simple compact shading. Avoid smooth edges, partial-cell triangles, tiny textures, gradients, blur, antialiasing, scenes, text and watermarks; use a transparent background.

Netherite edit: preserve the simplified iron silhouette, handle, centered latch, oak wood inset and transparency; replace iron metal with charcoal, warm brown-grey, muted purple-grey and dull grey netherite shading. No cyan, diamonds, gold or glow. Require whole cells on the same 16×16 grid, at most 12 flat colors, simple vanilla item shading, and no blur, gradients, antialiasing or text.
